Result & Inference
First Fit places each item into the first bin it fits, in the original order, so its bin count depends heavily on how the items arrive. Sorting items largest-first before running First Fit (First Fit Decreasing) tends to close gaps sooner, since big items claim space early instead of fragmenting bins later. Best Fit Decreasing goes further: for each item it scans every open bin and chooses the one that will be left with the least leftover space, which on this item set uses no more bins than the other two and often matches or beats them. All three stay within a small constant factor of the optimal bin count for this kind of greedy approach, while the exhaustive optimum would need far more computation to find.
